Reading this table
------------------

* 128 transpose reads and 512 WMMA survive every stage from 00_origin to the
  final ISA. Nothing is folded away, duplicated or lost in lowering -- which is
  the main thing you want an IR dump to tell you.
* The final ISA shows 128 ds_read_b128 AND 128 transposing reads: 256 LDS reads
  total, exactly matching the NT path's 256. The native NN pipeline swaps half
  the plain reads for transposing ones rather than adding to them.
* tr16 counts 0 at stage 19 because that stage is the serialised binary blob,
  not readable IR.
* 20_llvm_ir.ll shows 129/513 rather than 128/512: one extra match each from a
  declaration line rather than a call site.
* Many stages are byte-identical to their predecessor (noted above). Of 23
  files, only 13 are distinct -- part of why keeping all of them was poor value.
