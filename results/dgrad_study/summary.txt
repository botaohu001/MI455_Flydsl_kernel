A.  the fixed cost the linear model does not contain
============================================================================================================================================
t_eff(N) = t_gemm + t_transpose/N assumes the transpose and the GEMM cost the
same back to back as they do measured alone.  They do not: the GEMM waits on
the transpose, so each window pays one extra dependent-launch boundary.

  -- torch transpose --  model miss per *window*, us (should be ~constant in N if it is a launch boundary)
     N=1       N=2       N=4       N=8       N=16      N=32      
     3.3       3.9       6.1       13.2      25.0      39.4        <- median over 24 cells
     spread over cells: -5.7 to 122.3 us
